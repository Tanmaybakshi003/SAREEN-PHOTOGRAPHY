The Beginning

How It All Started

Arun Sareen picked up his first camera at nineteen, photographing friends' weddings on weekends while studying design in Delhi. What began as a hobby quickly became an obsession with light, timing and the quiet, unscripted moments that posed photographs never capture.

In 2012, Arun Sareen Photography opened its doors as a one-person studio with a single mission: to document real emotion, beautifully. Today, the studio has grown into a close-knit collective of photographers, cinematographers, retouchers and stylists — but that founding mission hasn't changed.

We've since photographed over 500 weddings, portraits, brand campaigns and events across India and internationally, building a style that blends documentary honesty with cinematic, editorial polish.

Our Philosophy

What Guides Our Work

Every shoot we take on is approached with the same set of principles, whether it's an intimate family portrait or a multi-day destination wedding.

Honest Storytelling

We document real moments as they unfold, blending candid emotion with thoughtfully composed portraits — never one at the expense of the other.

People First

A relaxed client feels comfortable in front of the camera. We invest time in getting to know you before the shoot, so the day feels easy, not staged.

Craft & Consistency

From color grading to album design, every deliverable goes through the same meticulous quality standard — no matter the size of the project.

Reliability

Clear communication, realistic timelines and a team that shows up prepared — every single time. Your day is too important for surprises.
