The First Frame

Arun shot his first wedding at nineteen, on a borrowed camera and a single roll of film. The images were imperfect — but the emotion wasn't. That feeling never left.

Wedding ceremony, early work
Bridal portrait series
2007

The Studio Takes Shape

Solo work grew into a collective — a cinematographer, a retoucher, a second shooter, all chasing the same instinct: no forced poses, no staged smiles. Just documentary honesty, elevated by craft.

Today

Stories Across the World

Today we shoot weddings from Udaipur to Tuscany, campaigns for emerging brands, and family portraits three generations will argue over. The gear has changed. The intent hasn't.
